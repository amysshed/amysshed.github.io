Girlhood

05/02/2026

Last night was my last session in my studio till early summer as I am setting off on some travels. There was a bittersweet feeling present in the room. This trip has been in the plans for a while now and it is very much needed. It's the jump that is needed to fully push me out of my known world. But, this January has been an amazing time for me creatively where I have spent a lot of time in this room and really feeling so action taking place.

Girlhood is a painting of my best pals inspired by a series of photos I captured when we were having pints at an Irish pub in Whitby Bay. I love to capture motion in my sketches and this felt like the first time I had a clear image to be able to capture motions using oil paint. It was a very exciting moment where I felt my creative pang in my chest the minute I captured the images.

So I began to explore different compositions the night I got home from Whitby Bay and started to paint the piece in early December. It was so nice to spend a long time on a piece, coming back and "hacking" away at different sections until the image began to form

I immediately wanted to transcribe the images into ink. Feel the motion of each direction, the soothing of each surge, and the sharpness of each peak. Here are two ink drawings I was inspired to create.
